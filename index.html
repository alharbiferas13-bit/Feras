Questions we get a lot

Is this against Roblox's rules? +

Trading in-game items for real money outside Roblox's official systems isn't something Roblox's Terms of Service permit, and it carries a real risk of account or item loss on both sides. We're upfront about that risk rather than hiding it — trade at your own discretion.

Do you ever need my password? +

Never. Fruit trades happen through Roblox's built-in trade feature while you're logged into your own account. Gamepass purchases are made by you, in-game, on your own account.

What if I don't receive my item? +

Payment is only released after you confirm delivery in our Discord order channel. If something goes wrong before that point, you don't pay.

How do I pay? +

Payment methods are shared in Discord once your order is confirmed. We only take payment after you've seen the item land in your inventory.
